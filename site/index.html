Methodology & Sensitivity

    
Baseline: 5★ = Promoter, 4★ = Passive, 1–3★ = Detractor. NPS = %Promoters − %Detractors (full precision, then rounded).
      Five stars is the clearest written expression of enthusiasm; four-star reviews often carry reservations; 1–3 stars signal dissatisfaction or a materially qualified experience.
      These boundaries are judgment calls. Results are a Review-Derived NPS, not survey NPS.

Selection bias & limitations

    
Online reviews overrepresent customers with unusually positive or negative experiences, creating a potentially bimodal distribution that exaggerates apparent customer polarization relative to the broader customer base.
